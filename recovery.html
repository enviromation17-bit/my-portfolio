Lead Recovery System

A six-stage workflow architecture designed to reduce the chance that a new lead gets lost between the first inquiry and the next action.

n8n workflow architectureAI-assisted analysisLead lifecycleHuman handoff
01 — INTAKE

Lead Intake

Receive and organize the incoming inquiry so the system has a consistent starting point.

02 — ANALYSIS

AI Lead Analysis

Evaluate the information, identify useful signals and prepare the lead for the appropriate next step.

03 — RESPONSE

Lead Response Generator

Prepare a context-aware customer response based on the lead and the workflow rules.

04 — OWNER

Owner Notification

Bring the business owner or team into the loop when a human needs visibility or action.

05 — FOLLOW-UP

Lead Follow-Up

Keep the opportunity moving with defined follow-up logic instead of relying on memory.

06 — STATUS

Lead Status Update

Maintain the lead lifecycle so the system knows what happened and what should happen next.

The complete recovery path

These six stages are designed as one connected lead recovery system, not six unrelated automations.
