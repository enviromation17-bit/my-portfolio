Lead Generation & Outreach Automation

A repeatable workflow for discovering businesses, filtering prospect data and preparing targeted outreach — designed to turn manual prospecting into a structured system.

System n8n + Python
Data Public business listings
Mode Demonstration

Find businesses by category and location

A workflow can query a chosen business category and city, collecting available names, locations and contact details into a structured dataset.

Stage 3 — Outreach

Prepare personalized communication

Once a prospect is qualified, the workflow can generate a tailored message, route it through an approval step when required, send through the chosen channel and record the result.
