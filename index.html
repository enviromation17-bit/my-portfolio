Think of Renolt as a helper that never forgets. When a customer messages your business, Renolt makes sure someone (or something) replies, follows up, and keeps track — so no customer gets ignored and no task falls through the cracks. We build these helpers, test them, and hand them to you ready to use.

How long does a typical project take?

Most single-problem systems are ready in 1–3 weeks once we understand your process. Bigger multi-step systems take longer, but we always start with one clear win.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. You explain how your business works today. We design, build, and test the system, then hand it over with simple instructions.

Will this replace my team?

No. Renolt handles the repetitive parts so your team can focus on the work that needs a human — decisions, relationships, and complex problems.

What if something goes wrong?

Every system is tested before it goes live. We also leave clear ways for a human to step in when needed.
