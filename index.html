The main fix

One system that catches every enquiry and follows up until it’s handled.

Renolt’s Lead System is a digital worker for your enquiries. It watches the channels that matter, logs every lead, sends a fast first reply when you want it, and alerts a human when it’s time to close.

What it does

  • Captures enquiries from WhatsApp, phone notes, website forms, and email
  • Replies with a clear, on-brand first message (you approve the wording)
  • Saves name, contact, source, and status in one place
  • Reminds your team when a lead needs a human call or visit
  • Keeps a simple record so nothing is “somewhere in chat”

You stay in control. The system does the remembering and the first step. Your people do the jobs and the closing.

FAQ

Straight answers

How long does this take?

Usually 1–3 weeks for a focused Lead System once we know your channels and reply rules.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You explain how enquiries come in today. We build and hand over simple steps.

Will this replace my staff?

No. It catches and organises enquiries. Your team still talks to customers and does the work.

What if a message needs a human right away?

You set rules. Urgent cases can alert a person immediately.

What channels can you connect?

We prioritise WhatsApp, website forms, email, and how you log phone enquiries. We only connect what you approve.

How much does it cost?

Depends on channels and complexity. Message us on WhatsApp with how enquiries come in now — we’ll give a clear range.

Can you build custom software — not only the Lead System?

Yes. Lead System is our main offer for service businesses, but we also build focused automation and internal tools when a different process is the real bottleneck. Describe the problem; we’ll say if it’s a good fit. See demos →

What do I need to prepare?

How customers reach you today, who replies, and where things get lost. Screenshots of WhatsApp or forms help. You don’t need a technical brief.
