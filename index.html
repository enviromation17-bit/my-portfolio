What is Renolt, in plain words?

Think of Renolt as a helper that never forgets. When a customer messages your business, Renolt makes sure someone (or something) replies, follows up, and keeps track — so no customer gets ignored and no task falls through the cracks. We build these helpers, test them, and hand them to you ready to use.

Questions people usually ask

Do I need to be technical to use this?

No. Renolt builds and sets everything up for you. You just use it — no coding, no complicated settings.

How long does it take to build?

It depends on the system, but we always start small with one process, get it working properly, and then grow from there.

What if I only have one small problem to fix?

That's the best place to start. Renolt is built around fixing one real process well, not selling you things you don't need.

Is my information safe?

Yes. We only connect the tools you approve, and important decisions always stay with a real person on your team.
