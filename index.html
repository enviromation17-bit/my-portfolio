The problem

This is not a marketing problem.
It’s a response problem.

You already get enquiries. They just die in the gaps between jobs, missed calls, and unread chats.

“I saw the message at 9pm.”

By then they already booked someone else.

“Form went to the wrong inbox.”

No one owns the lead. It sits for days.

“WhatsApp is a mess of threads.”

No status. No follow-up list. Notes lost.

The main fix

One system that catches every enquiry and follows up until it’s handled.

Renolt’s Lead System is a digital worker for your enquiries. It watches the channels that matter, logs every lead, sends a fast first reply when you want it, and alerts a human when it’s time to close.

  • Captures enquiries from WhatsApp, phone notes, website forms, and email
  • Replies with a clear, on-brand first message (you approve the wording)
  • Saves name, contact, source, and status in one place
  • Reminds your team when a lead needs a human call or visit

How it works

Four steps. No extra apps for your team to learn.

01

Enquiry arrives

WhatsApp, call, form, or email.

02

System catches it

03

Fast first action

Auto reply and/or alert to your team — rules you set.

04

Human closes

You call, quote, or book. The system tracks status.
